About the CORE chemical database

The Chemical Organization & Research Engine (CORE) brings together chemical information from curated datasets and external reference sources. It is being developed to make chemical identities, structures, and related information easier to connect and retrieve. Database coverage is expanding as information is collected and reviewed.

Information sources

CORE combines uploaded datasets with information gathered from external chemical reference services. The currently configured services include:

  • CAS Common Chemistry
  • PubChem
  • EPA CompTox Chemicals Dashboard
  • ChEMBL
  • NCI Chemical Identifier Resolver (CACTUS)
  • ChemSpider

Each source has different coverage and may report different details. A source may not provide every type of information for every chemical, and records continue to be added and assessed.

Information CORE is compiling

Chemical identifiers

Identifiers such as CAS Registry Numbers (CAS-RNs), EPA DTXSIDs, PubChem CIDs, ChemSpider IDs, FDA UNIIs, EINECS numbers, CERAPP IDs, and other identifiers supplied by sources.

Names and structures

Preferred and alternative names, molecular structures and their representations (including InChI, InChIKey, and SMILES), plus relationships between full structures, components, and parent structures where available.

Properties and source details

Molecular formula, molecular weight, exact mass, physicochemical properties, chemical classifications, and other source-reported chemical or biological information as it becomes available.

Information is kept with its source so that reported values can be traced and compared. Not every chemical record will contain every identifier, structure, property, or source report.
