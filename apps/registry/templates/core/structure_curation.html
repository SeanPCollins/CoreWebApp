Structure Curation

Scan Web Findings in the background, validate available chemistry, and preview unique InChIKeys with their supporting evidence.

Review before commit: scans and curation changes remain in temporary reports until you explicitly add accepted structures. That action writes Structure and StructureEvidence rows; deleting a report later does not undo those database changes. New WebFindings are placed in a persistent queue. Each normal batch samples a random, saved set, and findings not selected remain queued for later; use a full scan to process all currently pending findings.
